Making data

Hi, I'm Aravindhan Baskaran — a data analyst with a passion for turning messy, complex datasets into clean, decision-ready insights. I specialize in the full analytics pipeline: from raw data extraction and cleaning through to visualization and storytelling.

Over the past few years I've worked on projects spanning sales forecasting, customer behaviour analysis, supply-chain metrics, and financial KPI tracking — building dashboards in Power BI and Tableau, writing analytical pipelines in Python and SQL, and documenting findings that non-technical stakeholders can immediately act on.

I believe good data analysis is ultimately about reducing uncertainty. Every chart, every model, every dashboard should answer a real question and make the next step obvious.
